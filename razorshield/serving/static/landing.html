Catch the fraud. Explain every call.

RazorShield AI scores a payment the moment it happens, converts the probability into a 0–100 risk band, and shows the exact factors behind the decision — so an analyst can act on it and a customer can be told why.

From payment event to a decision an analyst can defend

Razorpay's webhook says a payment happened. It cannot say whether the customer is behaving normally — that history is ours to keep, and it is where the signal lives.

Payment event→ Signature verified→ 18 features from our event store→ Model→ SHAP→ Risk band + reason
1

Behaviour, not just the payment

Velocity, device age, deviation from the customer's own average and merchant dispute history — computed from history we maintain, because no gateway sends them.

2

A score with an action attached

Probability becomes a 0–100 score on a log-odds scale. Band cut-offs are fitted on validation data from what each band should mean, not picked as round numbers.

3

Reasons from SHAP, not invented

Contributions are additive and checked every run. The sentence is assembled from fixed templates — no language model is in the loop.

Measured on data the model never saw

Trained and evaluated on a controlled synthetic dataset; Razorpay Test Mode is the integration layer. The synthetic transactions are never presented as real Razorpay traffic.

Built so the numbers survive scrutiny

  • Split by time, never at random. A random split lets a customer's later transactions inform predictions about their earlier ones.
  • Chargebacks are lagged 35 days. A dispute raised on day 40 does not exist for a model scoring on day 20.
  • Selected on cost, not on F1. A missed fraud and a blocked customer are different currencies; the threshold follows the money.
  • Training and serving proven identical. A replay test asserts all 18 features match between batch and live, row by row.
Where it still fails

Two things it does not do, stated plainly:

  • Slow, low-value fraud is mostly missed. Amounts just above normal on the customer's own device leave almost no signal.
  • Genuine anomalies get flagged. A real customer on a new phone abroad looks identical to a takeover. That is the false-positive cost, and it is measured rather than hidden.

Score a payment in one call

The webhook is the production path. /score does the same work from a plain JSON body, so the system can be demonstrated without a Razorpay account at all.

Read the API reference 
# Score a payment attempt
curl -X POST http://localhost:8077/score \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{
    "transaction_id": "txn_001",
    "customer_id": "CUST_008736",
    "amount": 8450,
    "device_id": "DEV_NEW_9001"
  }'

{
  "risk_score": 83.0,
  "band": "CRITICAL",
  "summary": "Critical risk driven by a device
     first seen today and 17 transactions
     in the previous hour."
}
